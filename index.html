Udharo Khata Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Udharo Khata is a credit ledger (udharo khata) app for shopkeepers. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

What Udharo Khata stores

Udharo Khata stores your shop details, your customers' names and mobile numbers, the credit and payment entries you record, and any bill photos you add. All of it is stored only on your phone, inside the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Udharo Khata has no account, no server and no analytics. The developer cannot see, collect, sell or share your data.

Sending messages

When you tap a WhatsApp or Share button, Udharo Khata opens WhatsApp or another app you choose, with the message and any photos you selected. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself in that app. What happens next is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

Photos and camera

Udharo Khata uses the camera or photo picker only when you tap "Add bill photo". The photo is saved inside the app on your phone.

Payment details and QR codes

Your eSewa, Khalti, IME Pay or bank details and any payment QR images you add stay on your phone. They are put into a message or shared only when you tap Send or Share.

More than one shop

If you add more shops, each shop's khata is kept separately on the same phone. Backups include all shops.

Backups

When you tap "Make backup", Udharo Khata creates a file and lets you choose where to save or send it. If you set a backup password, the file is locked with it (AES-256 encryption) and cannot be opened without that password. Your app lock PIN is never put in a backup. Android may also include the app's data in your phone's own Google backup if you have turned that on.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, Udharo Khata asks for your PIN or fingerprint when it opens. Your PIN and recovery code are never saved as they are; the app keeps only a scrambled check of them on your phone. Fingerprint and face checks are done by Android, and Udharo Khata never sees your fingerprint or face.

Deleting your data

You can delete customers and entries inside the app. Uninstalling Udharo Khata deletes all of its data from your phone.

Children

Udharo Khata is meant for shopkeepers and is not directed at children.
